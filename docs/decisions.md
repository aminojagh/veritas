# Decisions that move a number

Every figure Veritas prints rests on a judgement somebody had to make: which cost
convention a profit is computed under, which of two dates a period filter keys on,
what counts as the same answer when accuracy is scored. **A number you cannot trace
is a number you should not trust** — that is the argument this project makes about
metric definitions, and it applies to our own figures first.

So this is the register. One row per decision that changes a number you will see —
in an answer, in a published evaluation table, or on the dashboard — naming the
decision, the number it moves, what to conclude when you meet that number, and the
dated review that argued it. **The reviews carry the evidence**: the command that
produced each figure, the date it was run, and the alternatives that were rejected.
Where a figure below could be moved by a later run, the register says which command
prints today's rather than repeating a number that will quietly stop being true.

Decisions that move no number a reader sees are in the working record at
[`.claude/docs/`](../.claude/docs/) instead. This is not a changelog.

## The numbers in an answer

What a Certified Metric means, and therefore what the App comes back with.

| Decision | The number it moves | What to conclude when you meet that number | Argued in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cost Basis uses average cost**, not first-in-first-out. | Realised P&L on every partial sale. | The sold quantity took its proportional share of what the whole holding cost. A first-in-first-out figure would differ, sometimes materially, and needs a lot ledger this schema deliberately does not carry — one Cost Basis is stored per holding. | [Sub-step 2.5](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-002-warehouse-and-ingestion.md#sub-step-25--generate-seeded-synthetic-client-activity), 2026-08-13 |
| **Realised P&L is gross of Commission.** | Realised P&L, and its relationship to Gross Revenue. | The client's profit is proceeds less what the sold share cost; the broker's Commission on the same sale is recognised separately, as revenue. Netting it into both would count one charge twice across two Certified Metrics. | [Sub-step 2.5](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-002-warehouse-and-ingestion.md#sub-step-25--generate-seeded-synthetic-client-activity), 2026-08-13 |
| **Every money metric answers in EUR**, converted row by row at the Foreign Exchange (FX) rate on that row's own date. | Every monetary figure — seven of the nine Certified Metrics. | A period total is the sum of separately converted rows, so it carries the rates of the days inside it rather than one closing rate. Trade Count and Position Change state no currency at all, being a count and a quantity. | [Sub-step 4.1](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-004-semantic-layer.md#sub-step-41--publish-the-semantic-entry-format-on-one-metric-definition), 2026-08-21 |
| **A Trade's period is its Trade Date**, a Snapshot metric's is the Snapshot date, and Realised P&L's is the Accounting Movement date. There is **no *by settlement date* axis**. | Every "in this period" figure. | *"Gross Revenue in the second quarter of 2026"* counts what was executed in the quarter, not what settled in it. The two are measurably different — 1.94% apart on that exact question, measured 2026-09-01 — and both are correct answers to different questions. Veritas certifies one of them and says which. | [Sub-step 4.5](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-004-semantic-layer.md#sub-step-45--write-the-dimension-definitions), 2026-08-25; measured in [Sub-step 7.1](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-007-evaluation.md#sub-step-71--write-the-gold-question-set), 2026-09-01 |
| **The Snapshot calendar is the intersection** of the traded instruments' trading calendars — a date carries Snapshots for every Instrument or for none. | Which dates an *"as of"* question can be asked about, and where a Position Change is attributed. | Sixty-six dates on which some market traded carry no Snapshot, so a change across one of them lands on the next Snapshot date instead (`check_warehouse.py --sources` prints both counts; sixty-six is the gap measured 2026-08-13). The alternative was marking a holding at a stale price, which is a fill-forward every later metric would inherit. | [Sub-step 2.5](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-002-warehouse-and-ingestion.md#sub-step-25--generate-seeded-synthetic-client-activity), 2026-08-13 |
| **The two movement tables carry opposite sign conventions**, and the schema says so beside the column. | Whether Net Revenue = Σcommission − Σrebate − Σfee reads true against the ledger. | `fct_cash_movement.amount` is signed from the Account's side — positive money enters it. `fct_accounting_movement.amount` is the magnitude recognised, always positive, with Realised P&L the one signed value there. A figure read out of one table under the other's convention has the wrong sign. | [Sub-step 2.5](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-002-warehouse-and-ingestion.md#sub-step-25--generate-seeded-synthetic-client-activity), 2026-08-13 |
| **Market Price is the unadjusted close**, booked on the exchange's own trading date and normalised to the major unit. | Every price, and every Position marked at one — Account Value, Unrealised P&L, Position Change. | A mark is the price the exchange actually printed that day. [`Adjusted Close`](../.claude/docs/glossary.md#c-distinctions-we-must-not-blur) is a back-adjusted series that rewrites history as later corporate actions occur: right for computing returns, forbidden for marking a Position. Each of the three readings it rejects — the adjusted series, the bar read on the wrong clock, the pence quote carried across as pounds — moves a measurable share of the price rows, and `check_warehouse.py --sources` prints how many under each on today's data. The same check fails the run if a day-over-day jump suggests a split inside the loaded window, because a real split would drop a mark with no trade behind it. | [Sub-step 2.3](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-002-warehouse-and-ingestion.md#sub-step-23--load-fct_instrument_price-from-yahoo-by-snapshot-and-replay), 2026-08-10 |
| **The book is nineteen instruments over a two-year window, and one seeded simulation of client activity.** | Every absolute total. | The aggregates are the size of *this* book, not of a business. Market data is real; clients, accounts, trades and movements are not, and the Accounts hold more cash than stock because each opening deposit is computed to cover the deepest hole that Account's own trading digs. Read the totals as internally consistent, never as realistic. | [Sub-step 2.2](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-002-warehouse-and-ingestion.md#sub-step-22--load-dim_instrument-from-nasdaq-trader-and-the-securities-and-exchange-commission-sec), 2026-08-10 and [Sub-step 2.5](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-002-warehouse-and-ingestion.md#sub-step-25--generate-seeded-synthetic-client-activity), 2026-08-13 |

## The numbers Veritas publishes about itself

What the evaluation tables and the dashboard mean.

| Decision | The number it moves | What to conclude when you meet that number | Argued in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Two results are the same answer within 0.01%** of the larger figure. | Execution Accuracy, and which questions the Gold Question Set is allowed to ask. | The tolerance absorbs rounding and a differently written but equivalent computation — not a different convention. It is also the width inside which a wrong answer would score as correct, so any pair of near-identical metrics a Gold Question turns on has to separate by more than it, and the test suite fails the run if one does not. | [Sub-step 7.1](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-007-evaluation.md#sub-step-71--write-the-gold-question-set), 2026-09-01 |
| **The corpus is indexed field by field, and a resolved meaning is spliced over the ambiguous word** rather than appended to the question. | The published retrieval table — Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR). | Both defaults were set by the sweep and not by taste: on the re-ranked search Veritas ships, the two settings together move MRR from 0.750 to 0.833, measured 2026-09-01. Read the table on MRR alone — hit rate is 1.000 in all sixteen cells at `top_k = 5`, so it decided nothing, and over twelve scored questions MRR moves in steps of 1/24. | [Sub-step 7.3](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-007-evaluation.md#sub-step-73--measure-retrieval-hit-rate-and-mrr), 2026-09-01 |
| **The shipped pair is the `rules` prompt and `gpt-5.4-mini`**, picked as the joint-best (model, prompt) combination of the published grid. | Every generation figure, and what a question costs. | The models axis separates and the prompts axis does not, so a change of model moves these numbers and a change of wording largely does not. The denominator is 23 of 24: one Gold Question — the `Account Value` one — is excluded, and derived rather than named, by asking the Validation Gate whether it would allow that question's own gold statement. | [Sub-step 8.1](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-008-observability.md#sub-step-81--choose-the-openai-default-model-by-measurement), 2026-09-03; published in [Sub-step 9.2](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-009-containerization-and-readme.md#sub-step-92--publish-the-generation-grid-over-openai-and-demote-groq), 2026-09-05 |
| **Temperature is pinned at zero — which is not determinism**, and the project measured that by accident. | Every published generation figure. | Each one is a single run. On 2026-09-05 the shipped model, prompt and committed questions scored one question apart in two runs six hours apart, and the same variance had been measured on 2026-09-03. So a one-question difference is noise, every rate has a margin nobody has quantified, and the differences these tables are actually read for are several times wider than that margin. | [Sub-step 8.1](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-008-observability.md#sub-step-81--choose-the-openai-default-model-by-measurement), 2026-09-03, and [Sub-step 9.2](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-009-containerization-and-readme.md#sub-step-92--publish-the-generation-grid-over-openai-and-demote-groq), 2026-09-05 |
| **A model call is costed at the vendor's list price on the day the page was read**, and a model no row prices costs `None` rather than zero. | The cost of a logged question, and the dashboard's cost column. | The column means *"what this would have cost at list prices"* — not an invoice. A call served on a free tier is billed at nothing and still carries its list price here, and nothing re-reads the vendors' pages, so a price that moved would produce figures that look exactly as authoritative as correct ones. A gap in the column is a model nobody has priced, which is a different thing from a call that cost nothing. | [Sub-step 8.3](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-008-observability.md#sub-step-83--record-every-question-a-person-asks), 2026-09-03; pages re-read in [Sub-step 9.3](../.claude/docs/reviews/step-009-containerization-and-readme.md#sub-step-93--readmemd-with-every-credential-and-every-limitation), 2026-09-05 |
